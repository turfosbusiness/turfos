The Current Feature Set

Built Around
the Work You Actually Do.

The details stay with the customer and property, so the team has useful context at every step.

TurfOS AI

Ask about your business and prepare changes to prices, schedules, customer records, and crew assignments. Review important changes before applying them.

Customers & Properties

Keep contacts, service locations, access notes, and pricing together. Import your existing customer spreadsheet and add new properties with a guided setup.

Property Maps

Find a service address, confirm the location, and outline the lawn. Use AI grass suggestions or draw manually, then review houses, driveways, and no-cut areas.

Schedules & Routes

Plan recurring visits, assign work, and organize route stops. Preview rain-day moves before changing your schedule.

Solo Owners & Crews

Work solo, mow alongside a team, or manage from the office. Employees use their own accounts to see assigned visits and property instructions.

Completion & Service History

Time visits, record the crew size, and attach completion photos. Keep the service date, cut pattern, mower setting, and property notes with the work.

Invoices & Customer Payments

Create invoices, send payment links, and track paid, processing, and unpaid statuses. Connect Stripe to accept available card and bank payment methods.

Flexible Billing

Choose billing after each service, weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. Scheduled billing groups completed work into property invoices when email automation is enabled.

Weather & Reminders

Use property forecasts to plan around rain and mowing conditions. Configured email reminders help customers prepare for upcoming visits.

Business Analytics

See recorded service totals, invoice statuses, and estimated versus actual service time. Follow the work with data from your own records.

Lawn Health & Equipment

Record problem areas and photos, keep crew instructions close, and manage mower settings. Use weather and mowing history as planning guidance.

Customer Portal

Customers sign in with email and password to view service information, invoices, and messages. Company signup links connect new customers to the right business.

TurfOS AI, online payments, and scheduled emails require setup. AI map suggestions are reviewed by your team; lawn-health records are observations, not automatic disease diagnoses.
