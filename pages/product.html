THE LAWN BECOMES THE SYSTEM

One property can hold a lot of information.
TurfOS keeps it together.

A property record brings together satellite imagery, editable boundaries, mowing and weed-wacking zones, slope, grass health, problem areas, service history and customer activity. Each visit can add new information that helps crews and office staff prepare for the next one.

THE FULL FEATURE SET

What TurfOS is designed to do.

One lawn-focused system for property setup, daily operations, service intelligence and customer care. These are the intended product capabilities as TurfOS is developed.

PRO CARE

Automatic property setup

Create a digital starting map of a customer's property from satellite imagery. The team can refine the map on site.

PRO CARE

Digital lawn maps

Show property boundaries, mowing zones, weed-wacking zones, slope, grass health, problem areas, previous mowing patterns and the recommended next pattern.

PRO CARE

Mowing history

Keep the last cut date and time, service duration, grass height, weather and pattern used with the property's record.

PRO CARE

Pattern intelligence

Track mowing direction and recommend a next pattern based on the lawn's history, with lawn health in mind.

PRO CARE · HOMEOWNER CARE

Weather intelligence

Bring together rain, temperature, humidity, soil moisture, wind, recent weather and upcoming forecast conditions.

HOMEOWNER CARE

“Should I mow today?”

Use current lawn and weather conditions to give homeowners practical guidance on whether it is a good time to mow.

PRO CARE

Grass height tracking

Use mower settings and recorded grass heights to track each cut and understand how it may affect the next mow.

PRO CARE · HOMEOWNER CARE

Photo-based lawn health

Record brown patches, bare areas, weeds, uneven growth, stress and possible disease so observations stay with the property.

PRO CARE

Mowing performance

See which lawns need service and which are complete. Keep service timing to help improve future planning.

PRO CARE

Scheduling and route management

Schedule service visits, organize customer locations into route stops, and track which lawns are due, in progress or complete.

PRO CARE

Customer portal, messages and invoices

Give customers direct access to service information, send messages and deliver invoices through the app.

Ideas under consideration
