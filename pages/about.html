WHY TURFOS

Lawn care has always been physical.
The software should understand that.

TurfOS is built for lawn-care businesses. It connects service schedules and routes to each property's conditions, work history and customer updates, keeping the workday and the lawn in the same system.

T01
THE IDEA

Start with the lawn.
Build everything around it.

Every property has its own conditions, history, patterns, weather and needs.

TurfOS brings those pieces together so lawn-care teams can plan service, give crews useful property context and keep customers informed about the work being done.

THE TURFOS MODEL
PROPERTY + WEATHER + WORK + HISTORY + CUSTOMER
