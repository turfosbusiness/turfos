A Few Helpful Details

Before You Get Started.

Can I Use TurfOS as a Solo Owner?

Yes. The workspace supports owners who mow on their own, owners who mow alongside a team, and owners who manage crews.

Do Customers Need a Company Code Every Time?

No. Customers and employees sign in with email and password. The code or signup link is used when they join the business.

Can I Choose When to Bill Customers?

The platform supports billing after each service, weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. Scheduled email delivery needs to be configured.

Are Payment and AI Services Automatically Connected?

These services need setup. Ask about the configuration and any service charges that apply to your business.
